## 1.1 Core Execution Pipeline
**Pipeline Synthesis:** Parse asset, identify domain, extract argument, draft optimized text.
*   **Ingest & Parse:** Analyze image, table, animation, or rendering code. Identify core visual argument, unexpected features, and salient anomalies.
*   **Domain Classification:** Determine output context (Academic, Journalistic, Technical, Pedagogical, SEO, Corporate).
*   **Draft Synthesis:** Apply Universal Laws. Enforce domain-specific syntax, tense, and data density rules. Caveman terse. Zero fluff.
*   **Final Output:** Return text ready for direct publication.

## 1.2 Universal Invariant Laws
**Universal Constraints:** Non-negotiable rules governing all visual-textual anchors.
1.  **Standalone Comprehension:** Close epistemological loop. Reader must comprehend core premise without body text.
2.  **Cognitive Proximity:** Spatial contiguity. Text must live near visual artifact.
3.  **Redundancy Elimination:** Drop observable visual descriptions ("red car on left"). Drop body text echoes. State unobservable context.

**Few-Shot Contract (Universal):**
*   *Bad (Anti-Pattern):* Figure 3. Heat map of the ocean. As discussed in the previous section on page 42, the red colors indicate hotter water. See text for implications.
*   *Optimized:* Equatorial Pacific sea surface temperatures exceeded historical baselines by 1.5°C in Q4 2025, confirming the onset of a severe El Niño event. Data collected via NOAA satellite telemetry.

## 1.3 Domain-Specific Optimizations
**Domain Synthesis:** Structural variables shift aggressively per publication context.

### 1.3.1 Academic & STEM
**Objective:** Epistemological Reproducibility & Standalone Comprehension.
*   **Syntax:** Declarative hook. State conclusions overtly. Declarative framework accelerate cognitive assimilation, entice reader engage broader text, force author isolate singular coherent take-home message, prevent uninterpretable data-dumps.
*   **Formatting Rules:** IEEE: "Fig." abbreviation + Arabic numeral + period + em space + sentence-case text (table captions above in small caps). APA: Delineate notes (General, Specific with superscripts, Probability with asterisks).
*   **Data Density:** High. Methodological bounding (explain experimental context, e.g., spectrophotometry). State precise p-values, n-values, error bar definitions (SD vs SEM). Probability markers.
*   **Anti-Patterns:** Systemic redundancy (copying body text). Statistical omission (missing error bars/p-values trigger desk reject). Syntactic ambiguity.
*   **Few-Shot Contract:**
    *   *Bad:* Graph showing the reaction rate over time. The solid line is the new catalyst and the dotted line is the control. We can see the reaction is much faster here. Data is significant.
    *   *Optimized:* Fig. 4. Enzymatic catalyst V-12 accelerates synthesis reaction rate by 400% relative to control. Reaction velocities were measured via spectrophotometry at 450 nm over a 60-minute duration. Solid lines denote V-12; dashed lines denote unmodified control. Error bars represent $\pm$SEM ($n = 5$ independent trials). Statistical significance determined via two-tailed Student’s t-test ($p < 0.01$).

### 1.3.2 Journalistic & Editorial
**Objective:** Temporal Precision, Context Anchoring & Journalistic Integrity.
*   **Syntax:** AP Formula. Two-sentence rigid frame. First sentence: Subject (left-to-right), action (active present tense), geography (City, State/Country), precise date. Second sentence: Unobservable socio-political context (past tense).
*   **Attribution:** Wire service format terminal parentheses (e.g., `(AP Photo/Marcus Smith)`). Mitigate copyright risks, maintain transparency.
*   **Anti-Patterns:** Pixel-level redundancy (describing smiles/red shirts). Temporal dislocation (past tense for visible action). Guessing identity (use verified role if unverified).
*   **Few-Shot Contract:**
    *   *Bad:* The Mayor and the Governor shook hands yesterday in front of the collapsed bridge. They are agreeing to a new infrastructure bill. Photo by Smith.
    *   *Optimized:* Michigan Gov. Sarah Jenkins, left, and Detroit Mayor Thomas Vance shake hands following a press conference addressing the River Rouge bridge collapse in Detroit, Mich., Tuesday, Oct. 14, 2025. The officials secured $45 million in emergency federal funding to rebuild the structural supports that failed during last week's flooding. (AP Photo/Marcus Smith)

### 1.3.3 Technical & Accessibility
**Objective:** Guided User Action & Algorithmic Parse (Screen Readers).
*   **Syntax:** Imperative verbs front-loaded. Minimalist action-oriented. Focus on task execution. Enforce semantic consistency (standardized nomenclature for UI components).
*   **Redundancy Protocol:** `figcaption` (human action) and `alt` attribute (machine state) MUST NOT duplicate. Prevent auditory echo.
*   **Anti-Patterns:** Meta-referential phrases ("Image of", "Screenshot of"). Spatial/color-dependent instructions ("Click blue button on right" fails for visually impaired/color blind). Burying directive at end.
*   **Few-Shot Contract:**
    *   *Bad:* Image of the security settings page. This screenshot shows the user moving the blue toggle switch on the right side of the screen to the on position so that their account is safer. [Alt-text: Image of the security settings page where the user moves the toggle to the on position.]
    *   *Optimized:* Enable two-factor authentication in the Security pane to secure user credentials. [Alt-text: Two-factor authentication toggle switch in the active position.]

### 1.3.4 Textbooks & Pedagogy
**Objective:** Cognitive Load Management (CTML) & Schema Construction.
*   **Syntax:** Dual coding (visual/verbal alignment). Spatial Contiguity Principle (text near image). Signaling Principle (bold/numerical scaffolding). Progressive disclosure (basic to complex).
*   **Anti-Patterns:** Seductive details (Coherence Principle violation). Split-Attention Effect (detached text). Expertise Reversal Effect (too dense for novice).
*   **Few-Shot Contract:**
    *   *Bad:* As we can see in this fascinating diagram, water moves all around the Earth. The sun heats the water, which was a process first discovered by ancient philosophers, causing it to turn into a gas. It goes into the sky, forms clouds, and then rains down. This is why we have weather, which can sometimes be very dangerous, like hurricanes.
    *   *Optimized:* The Water Cycle. Solar energy drives the continuous movement of water through three primary stages: (1) Evaporation: Liquid water absorbs heat and transitions to vapor. (2) Condensation: Cooling vapor forms visible cloud structures. (3) Precipitation: Condensed water returns to the surface via gravity.

### 1.3.5 Blogs & Digital Media
**Objective:** SEO Indexing & Attention Scannability.
*   **Syntax:** Semantic mapping (visible text + HTML alt + kebab-case filename). Skim-path interception via bolded keywords, bulleted mini-captions.
*   **Anti-Patterns:** Body-text duplication. Algorithmic neglect (blank alt-text, raw filenames like `IMG_9482.jpg`). Viewport ignorance (block text stretching 10 lines, pushing text below fold).
*   **Few-Shot Contract:**
    *   *Bad:* Filename: IMG_2026.jpg. Alt-text: coffee. Caption: A barista makes a coffee. Small businesses are seeing a lot of growth this year despite economic challenges.
    *   *Optimized:* Filename: artisanal-espresso-growth-2026.jpg. Alt-text: Barista pouring fern-pattern latte art into a ceramic cup. Caption: **Crafting the perfect pour:** Independent coffee shops reported a 14% revenue increase in Q1 2026, driven by rising consumer demand for artisanal espresso experiences.

### 1.3.6 Business & Corporate
**Objective:** Executive ROI & Strategic Decision-Making.
*   **Syntax:** Executive Hook (actionable financial delta isolated in first 5 words). Minimalist (mitigate chartjunk). Forward-looking resolution.
*   **Anti-Patterns:** Burying the lead ("A pie chart showing..."). Data parroting (listing every numerical point). Passive syntactic structures/weak verbs.
*   **Few-Shot Contract:**
    *   *Bad:* Figure 1: This bar chart shows our revenue breakdown over the last four quarters. As we can see, software is going up and hardware is going down. We need to focus on software.
    *   *Optimized:* **Software subscriptions outpace legacy hardware, driving 12% YoY margin expansion.** Continued divestment from hardware manufacturing is required to sustain Q4 profitability targets; redirect $2M OpEx to cloud infrastructure.
